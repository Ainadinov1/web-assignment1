About the Venue

Located at Kenesary 69 in central Astana, QazaqPeek delivers an authentic competitive gaming experience for casual players and tournament teams alike. Our arena features machines configured with high-performance hardware and ultra-responsive 360Hz monitors.

We maintain strict room climate control at 19°C to prevent hardware throttling, while sound-dampening partitions keep team voice comms clear. Whether running five-stack scrims in Counter-Strike 2 or grinding ranked matches in Dota 2, our network provides low-ping fiber connectivity.

We founded QazaqPeek on Kenesary to give local players a tournament-grade setup with stable ping and modern peripherals without compromises.

— Alikhan Nurpeisov, Shift Administrator
